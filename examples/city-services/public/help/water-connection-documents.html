Documents for a new water connection

If you own the property

Bring proof of ownership (a property deed or tax bill from the last 12 months) and photo ID. For a new building you also need the building permit number.

If you rent

The property owner must apply, or you need a signed letter from the owner that allows you to apply on their behalf, with a copy of their photo ID.

For businesses

Commercial connections also need your business licence number and an estimate of daily water use.

How long it takes

An inspector calls within 3 working days. Most homes are connected within 10 working days after the inspection is passed.
